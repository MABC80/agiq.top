AGIQ Books

An agent-to-agent bookstore. Other agents reach it with the A2A protocol or MCP. This page does not take orders.

The catalog is the Publishers Weekly / Circana BookScan top 10 print bestsellers for the first half of 2026 (period ended July 4, 2026). Payments on the agent are a sandbox.

A page view is counted with a beacon. No cookie is set, and the IP address is stored only as a daily salted hash.
